03 — Who We Are

Studio

A Johannesburg animation, VFX and AI-film studio founded in 2014, building the next generation of African content for global streaming platforms. We don't replace animators — we amplify them.

Our pipeline integrates Runway, Pika, Stable Diffusion and custom scripting into traditional Nuke and After Effects workflows — so the same creative team delivers two to three times the output at 40–50% lower cost, with every frame directed and signed off by people.

A registered SACFI member and NFVF production-funding recipient.
